# Everyday jacket care

Product ID: everyday-jacket
Applies to: Everyday jacket, all colors and sizes in the store.

## Washing and drying

Close the zipper before washing. Machine wash on a gentle cycle at 30 degrees Celsius with similar colors. Do not use bleach or fabric softener. Hang to dry. Do not tumble dry or iron.

## Limits

These instructions apply only to this product. No stain-removal treatment is documented. If the customer's garment label differs, follow the garment label and ask the store to clarify the mismatch.
