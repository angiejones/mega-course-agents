# Leather jacket care

Product ID: leather-jacket
Applies to: Leather jacket, all colors and sizes in the store.

## Cleaning and storage

Do not machine wash or tumble dry. Take the jacket to a specialist leather cleaner. Store on a padded hanger away from direct heat.

## Limits

These instructions apply only to this product. No home stain-removal treatment is documented. If the customer's garment label differs, follow the garment label and ask the store to clarify the mismatch.
