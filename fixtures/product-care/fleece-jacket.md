# Fleece jacket care

Product ID: fleece-jacket
Applies to: Fleece jacket, all colors and sizes in the store.

## Washing and drying

Turn the jacket inside out. Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle. Do not use fabric softener. Lay flat to dry. Do not iron.

## Limits

These instructions apply only to this product. No stain-removal treatment is documented. If the customer's garment label differs, follow the garment label and ask the store to clarify the mismatch.
